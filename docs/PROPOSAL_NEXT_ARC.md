## Review outcome — the recommendation reversed

The original recommendation (CW 11 first) was overturned by the cross-vendor
review on an argument this author accepts:

1. **CW 11 is not an independent falsification register.** Jung frames
   alchemy as a projection of and compensation for Christian dogma — the two
   ontologies are intertwined, and this graph already carries the bridges
   (christ/lapis, the-self/filius-regius). Testing alchemical vocabulary on
   theology tests a system against its own mirror. The clean second register
   is CW 8's clinical-structural mechanics.
2. **CW 8 is the natural successor to 9i**: it holds the theoretical bedrock
   ("On the Nature of the Psyche", psychic energy, synchronicity) for exactly
   the phenomenological nodes 9i is minting now, and archetype's existing
   references already make CW 8 its plurality home.
3. Conceded from the remaining findings: the incidental-reference table is
   directional, not predictive (single-digit counts); the CW 11 cost line
   must price a minting-heavy register, not extrapolate 9i attachment
   economics; and evidence-mix dilution is an observation the strips report,
   not a goal that picks volumes.
4. One actionable improvement regardless of ordering: CW 11's mining rules
   must carry an explicit exegesis clause (Jung reporting doctrine vs
   asserting) beyond the standing claim_type line — the reviewer, who runs
   this pipeline's audits, reports authorial-endorsement hallucination as the
   dominant failure mode in religious text.

**Revised recommendation: CW 8 next (fourteen windows, §1–997), CW 11 after
it with a minting-aware budget and the exegesis rule, CW 5 and 16 close the
corpus.** The original argument below is retained unedited for the record.

## Recommendation: CW 11, then CW 8

**CW 11 next**, for three reasons in order of weight:

1. **It is the second falsification register for the transfer study.** v1.5's
   headline is whether the relation vocabulary and families, built on
   alchemical material, transfer. CW 9i tests archetypal theory without
   alchemical carriers; CW 11 tests the doctrinal-theological register —
   Trinity, Mass, Job, East/West practice. Two independent non-alchemical
   registers make the transfer claim falsifiable twice, from different
   directions. CW 16 cannot serve here: its transference essay is a Rosarium
   commentary, alchemy-adjacent by construction — it deepens, it does not
   test.
2. **Aion just built its receiving structure.** The graph's religious spine
   (christ 85 edges, god-image 36, quaternity 22, antichrist, holy-ghost) is
   CW 9ii's work of the last two days, and CW 11 is where Jung argues that
   material at book length. Expect the attach-vs-mint ratio to be the
   arc's most informative metric: high attachment onto Aion nodes would be
   structural confirmation that two volumes written years apart describe one
   system — the strongest kind of transfer evidence the project can produce.
   The Concordance's evidence-mix strips make this visible to readers as it
   happens.
3. **It dilutes the known evidence skew where it hurts most.** the-self (96
   edges) and christ (85) currently lean on CW 9ii + trilogy; anima is 80%
   alchemically evidenced, the-unconscious 78%. CW 11 adds a third
   independent tradition of evidence under nodes the atlas presents as
   central.

**CW 8 follows** (the theoretical register: psychic energy, synchronicity,
the archetype paper itself — archetype's 9 incidental refs already make CW 8
its plurality home). Sequencing it after CW 11 gives the transfer study three
registers at v1.5 close, or holds it for v1.6 if capacity says so.

CW 5 and CW 16 close the corpus later; neither carries a falsification
argument the study needs now.

## Shape and cost

Fourteen windows of ~73 paragraphs (§1–1016). At measured rates ($0.09–0.16
per verified citation, b-cw9i-1 = $10–11/window): **~$130–160 for the arc.**
Same chain, same blinding, same form rule, one pass per window, family
assignment folded into each merge per the standing 6b step. The
`jungkg-cw9i-mining` task retires when 9i completes; a `jungkg-cw11-mining`
task with identical structure replaces it.

## Risks, named

- **Register shift may spike minting** (new theological vocabulary: Mass
  liturgy, Eastern practice, Job's frame). That is the experiment working,
  not failing — but the window plan should expect lower reuse early and the
  budget line above assumes 9i-like density, which CW 11 may exceed.
- **Attribution pressure.** CW 11 is thick with Jung *reporting* doctrine —
  claim_type discipline (jung-reports-parallel vs jung-asserts) will be
  stressed harder than anywhere yet. The gate already checks this; the
  cross-vendor referent checks matter more here than in 9i.
- **Fatigue on the human queue.** Fourteen windows of family assignment will
  add disagreements to the review queue while 153 already wait. Mitigation:
  the queue is drip-fed and the arc does not gate on adjudication.
