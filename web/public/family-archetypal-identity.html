Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 4. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

The text asserts the two terms are ONE — via the projection thesis: a single archetype behind both terms (Edinger, Anatomy of the Psyche; the answer to the literal-absurdity objection). Includes scoped identity where a qualifier restricts the claim. Symmetric in principle; synonym-of carries the owl:SymmetricProperty precedent.

Boundary: the strongest family — demand the text's own identity assertion of the TERMS (see the indistinguishability rule above). Watch identity overreach, the E-series' recurring catch.
