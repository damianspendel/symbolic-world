Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 13. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

The subject brings forth or begets the object — psychological or mythological parenthood, the parent persisting beside the offspring.

Boundary: not transformation; not derivation (historical/doctrinal descent is family 14, split out at v0.2 — the same verb "originates" serves both, so read the terms: traditions derive, beings beget); kinship ROLES (brother, consort) are family 20.

Inverse pair declared: produces / originates-in (alongside produces/produced-by) — projection originates-in the-unconscious (9ii §17) and god-image originates-in psychoid (14 §787) are generation, inverse direction; only tradition-to-tradition originates-in (per §14's relation test) is derivation.
