Vocabulary

the terms of the RDF export

These terms describe what the atlas records about a claim: where it rests, whose voice it is in, how strongly it is committed, and which checks it passed. They are built on PROV-O. They say nothing about Jung's concepts: there is no class hierarchy over the nodes, no domain or range on any relation of the graph, no transitivity, and nothing is inferred from a node's type.

The namespace https://w3id.org/symbolicworld/ is provisional and not yet registered, so these terms do not resolve yet. The prefix sw: stands for https://w3id.org/symbolicworld/ns#.

Classes

sw:Node

A named thing in the graph. Its type is a coarse label, and nothing is inferred from it.

sw:Relation

One stored edge: a subject, a relation and an object. The family is filed here. The rdf:predicate is the effective relation, which is the normalized form where one exists.

subclass of rdf:Statement · subclass of prov:Entity

sw:Claim

One statement backed by one passage. A relation has one claim per citation, and the gate rules on each claim separately.

subclass of prov:Entity

sw:Reference

The place a claim rests on: a volume and a paragraph of the Collected Works. The export does not carry the quoted words, which are on the concept page for the claim.

subclass of prov:Entity

sw:Stage

One recorded step of the pipeline, dated where the record gives a date. Passing one stage warrants nothing about the next.

subclass of prov:Activity

sw:Proposal

A model drafted something. For a claim it is the mining batch, linked by prov:wasGeneratedBy, and sw:attributionSource says whether a batch record or the documented procedure names the proposer. Claims whose audit names no batch have none.

subclass of sw:Stage

sw:GateCheck

A model of a different family from the proposer judged the paragraph against the claim, or a normalized form against its claim.

subclass of sw:Stage

sw:CrossVendorAudit

A model from another company judged the work again. The verdict is given as recorded, and an audit that records none has none here.

subclass of sw:Stage

sw:FamilyAssignment

An assignment of a relation to a family, or a record that none was settled. A family is assigned, never verified.

subclass of sw:Stage

sw:Voice

Whose voice the claim is in. This is a separate question from whether the claim is true.

sw:Commitment

How strongly the claim is committed, from the confidence field of the reference.

sw:Rung

A warrant: the specific backing a part of a claim has. The rungs have no order in this vocabulary, because passing one warrants nothing about another.

sw:FamilyState

Where a relation stands in the family layer.

Voice

sw:JungAsserts

Jung says it himself.

sw:JungReportsParallel

Jung reports someone else's material as a parallel.

sw:JungQuotesSource

Jung quotes a source, which is then named.

Commitment

sw:CommitmentHigh

The reference records its confidence as high.

sw:CommitmentMedium

The reference records its confidence as medium. A record of high -> medium also lands here, with the original string kept beside it.

sw:CommitmentLow

The reference records its confidence as low.

Rung

sw:Attested

The quote is in the paragraph. It settles only that the words are in the book.

sw:ModelSupported

A gate of a different family from the proposer passed the claim.

sw:CrossChecked

A model from another company judged the claim again. Read the audit's verdict beside this rung.

sw:Normalized

The relation has a short normalized form beside the frozen wording.

sw:FamilyAssigned

The relation is filed under a family. It inherits nothing from the verification of the claim beneath it.

sw:Derived

A row produced at query time by a declared inverse or symmetry rule. Derived rows are never stored, so this rung has no instances in the export.

sw:HumanReviewed

One person judged it. The review records sit outside the graph's source data, so this export reports no instances.

sw:HumanAgreed

Two people judged it independently. No item in the graph has reached this rung.

Family state

sw:StateAssigned

A family is on record.

sw:StatePendingAdjudication

The arms disagreed, and the relation waits for a person to rule.

sw:StateNoFamily

The relation is carried by no family.

sw:StateTextUndecided

The text does not decide between families.

A recorded stage that acted on this claim or relation.

subproperty of prov:wasInfluencedBy · takes sw:Stage

sw:family

The family this relation is filed under, named by its super-property. The assignment belongs to the edge, not to the relation string.

on sw:Relation

sw:familyState

Where the relation stands in the family layer.

on sw:Relation · takes sw:FamilyState

sw:verbatimRelation

The frozen wording the gate judged, where a normalized form replaced it as the effective relation. The export does not carry it, because a long predicate can repeat the source's words.

on sw:Relation

sw:nodeType

The node's coarse type label, as recorded.

on sw:Node · takes xsd:string

sw:volume

The volume of the Collected Works, as recorded: 9i, 14.

on sw:Reference · takes xsd:string

sw:paragraph

The numbered paragraph.

on sw:Reference · takes xsd:integer

sw:quote

The quoted words. The export does not carry them, and a switch in the exporter can write them to a separate file outside the CC BY grant.

on sw:Reference · takes xsd:string

sw:voiceSource

The source named for the claim's voice, as recorded.

on sw:Claim · takes xsd:string

sw:confidenceAsRecorded

The original confidence string, given where it is a downgrade rather than a single level.

on sw:Claim · takes xsd:string

sw:verdict

The audit's verdict as recorded. Older audits use different words, and they are not merged here.

on sw:CrossVendorAudit · takes xsd:string

sw:batch

The mining batch named in a stage: the batch an audit names, or the batch a proposal is.

on sw:Stage · takes xsd:string

sw:auditRecord

The audit record as stored. The agent, date, verdict and batch here are read from this string.

on sw:CrossVendorAudit · takes xsd:string

sw:proposerAsRecorded

The words that name the proposer, as the source gives them: a model id, or a family such as Opus-family miner subagent.

on sw:Proposal · takes xsd:string

sw:attributionSource

Where the proposer comes from: record, when a batch record names it, or procedure, when only the documented mining procedure does.

on sw:Proposal · takes xsd:string

sw:assignedBy

How the family was assigned, as recorded: the two arms agreeing, or a person ruling.

on sw:FamilyAssignment · takes xsd:string

sw:familyAudit

The calibrated gate's audit of the assignment, as recorded.

on sw:FamilyAssignment · takes xsd:string

sw:recordedAs

The string the graph's source data stores for this individual.

Relation families

The families are object properties under https://w3id.org/symbolicworld/family/. A verbatim relation sits under a family only if its string is in exactly one family. Each family has its own page, with its definition.
