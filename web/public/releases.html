Releases

What changed between one release of the graph and the next, and why. The ledger records every individual claim and the gates it passed; this file records the release-level story.

Every entry has the same four sections in the same order — mining status, governance, core build, UI — so two releases can be read against each other line for line.

Every entry opens with a version pin. A graph release and a ruleset version are deliberately separate numbers — a release pins the ruleset it ships with rather than sharing its number — and the pin names the calibration record that was binding at tag time.

Pointers. Artifacts inside the repository are named as repo-relative paths in code formatting: the repository is not public until the ceremony, so they are paths today and become links when it is. Pages on the site are given as real links, extensionless by the site's own convention.

This file carries no typed figure. Counts are written as named tokens in double braces and bound from the graph, the calibration records and the committed experiment receipts by web/build-atlas.mjs; a token the build cannot bind fails the build rather than shipping as literal braces.

v1.4 — the alchemical trilogy, the archetype volumes, and the relation-family layer

pending — the ceremony waits on the arXiv publication of the paper. The ruleset in the pin is whichever version is binding at tag time; v0.7.1 is binding today, and the v0.7.x line is still moving. Scope note: the plan of 2026-08-05 put Aion and the archetype volumes in v1.5 and kept v1.4 to the trilogy. The arcs closed faster than the ceremony did, so this entry describes the graph as it stands rather than the plan as it was written, and v1.5 is re-scoped to the study the arcs were mined for.
graph v1.4 · ruleset v0.7.1 · calibration record pipeline/family_calibration/records/2026-08-13_v071_run6.json (PASS)

mining status

  • Five arcs mined end to end since v1.3.0: Psychology and Alchemy (CW 12), Alchemical Studies (CW 13) and Mysterium Coniunctionis (CW 14) complete the alchemical trilogy; Aion (CW 9ii §1–429) closed on 2026-08-06 with b-cw9ii-7; The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious (CW 9i §1–718) closed on 2026-08-12 with b-cw9i-10, ten windows, every reference proposed by an Opus-family model, gated by a Fable-family model and audited across vendors by Gemini.
  • 407 nodes, 1555 edges and 1847 references over 51 merged batch entries. Every reference carries a verbatim quote of at most 25 tokens and the paragraph it came from; each batch's own account of what it merged is on its delta page, and every claim is on the ledger.
  • CW 9i was mined as the transfer study's falsification volume: archetype theory without alchemical carriers, against a graph in which a large share of nodes had rested on the trilogy alone. What that did to the evidence mix is on each node's panel in the atlas, not summarised into a claim here.
  • Relation strings normalized on 260 edges. The verbatim string stays frozen beside the normalized one, and the family layer classifies the normalized form where it exists.
  • The family layer covers the graph: 1250 assigned, 262 waiting on a human ruling, 41 that no family covers, 2 where the sentence itself hedges. An assignment classifies a relation that was already gated against its paragraph. It is not a second verification, and nothing on this site calls a family verified. Family by family: relation families.
  • Defect found in the pipeline: the clause-predicate shape. The windowed pipeline built after v1.3.0 shipped carried no rule about the shape of a relation. Every gate asked whether the paragraph supported the claim; none asked whether the predicate was a predicate. The result was 271 relations whose predicate carries a whole proposition — found, named and measured on 2026-08-05, starting exactly at b-cw12-1.
  • The mechanism was a line in the miner's own standing briefing instructing that qualifiers stay inside the relation string. A watched batch tidied its relations in spite of it; the blind b-cw9ii-2 followed it literally and came back at 77% clause predicates. That is a defect that propagated through the instruction channel, not through the model.
  • Remediation. The bullet was replaced with a form rule — the relation is a verb phrase of at most three hyphen tokens; carrier, scope, condition and modality belong in qualifiers or the object. b-cw9ii-3 was the test, and every blind batch since has run at 0% clause predicates: fifteen consecutive by the close of CW 9i on 2026-08-12. The affected relations were not re-mined — each is individually verified and true, the shape is wrong and not the fact — so they carry a normalization layer beside the frozen original, proposed, gated and sampled through the same chain as any other claim.

governance

  • docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md reached v0.7.1, holding 25 families. The version is binding only while a calibration PASS record for it is committed under pipeline/family_calibration/records/; the version↔calibration handshake in tools/check_paper_facts.py refuses a version without one.
  • The re-run of the amplified-by edges under ruling 1 classified those relations by the content of their quotes rather than by the layer tag they carried. Where the amplification family now stands is on its own page, count included, whatever that count has become.
  • 7 relation declarations are active — inverse and symmetric pairs, each transcribed from a line of the definitions document into pipeline/family/relation_declarations.json and applied at read time only. No declaration asserts transitivity. The rulings behind them, and what the ontology deliberately does not contain, are on the ontology page.
  • The family gate was re-calibrated against planted errors under v0.7.1: 19 of 19 planted errors caught with the right family named, 40 of 41 untouched assignments left alone (pipeline/family_calibration/records/2026-08-13_v071_run6.json). A caught planted error is evidence about the instrument, not about Jung, and the record covers only the error classes we know how to plant. The gate model is gemini-3.1-pro-preview — the same model family as one of the two production classification arms, which is within-vendor role separation and not a cross-vendor check, and the record says so.
  • The external-validity check closed on 2026-08-12 over 3 passes against 8 works of secondary scholarship. Of 16 sampled assignments the literature treats, scholars' renderings agreed with our family on 8, disagreed on 6 and split among themselves on 2; 6 pairs no consulted work treats at all. 16 treated pairs is a small sample, and agreement there is not endorsement: no scholar reviewed this vocabulary. The disagreements produced 7 rule changes and are published beside the agreements, family by family. Sample, per-item worksheets and both review passes: docs/experiments/external_validity_2026-08/.
  • CI gates in force at this release, all in tools/check_paper_facts.py unless named otherwise: paper counts pinned to the v1.3.0 tag and living-doc counts to head; the version↔calibration handshake above; five family checks; the ledger-freshness gate and the batch-manifest gate in tools/pages/preflight.py; and the copy contract in web/build-atlas.mjs, which fails the build on a count-like literal that the graph cannot account for. Rules are registered in docs/RULES.md.
  • Corrected or withdrawn. Four treatments supplied by a reviewing model in the external check's first pass were put to the actual texts: one held, one was retracted, and two named headwords the source does not contain. None entered the headline rates; they are named here because they appeared in a draft that was circulated. Calibration figures published before August 2026 were withdrawn when a code inspection found they could not be substantiated — the sections that carried them stand in the definitions document as a record of what was done, not as current evidence. And the b-cw9ii-1 / b-cw9ii-2 comparison is confounded: the blinding of the miner changed between them, so the pair is not clean evidence about the material, and the 77% above is what that batch did rather than a measurement of anything else.

core build

  • The site went live on Cloudflare Pages on 2026-08-07 at symbolicworld.observer, with the frozen v1.3.0 snapshot preserved at /v1.3/ and linked from the landing page, since the paper describes that release and not this one. Runbook: tools/pages/RUNBOOK.md.
  • Public URLs are extensionless by convention from 2026-08-10; the sitemap, the canonical tags and every internal link agree on that form.
  • The generators are deterministic: the same graph in gives the same bytes out, so a real change is not buried in churn. web/build-atlas.mjs writes the atlas projection, the family map, the pages and the sitemap; tools/build_ledger_page.py writes the ledger and one delta page per batch in the same pass.
  • Data artifacts emitted at build: the public dataset at /graph.json, the declarations at web/public/relation_declarations.json, the batch-page manifest at web/public/batch_pages.json (which the deploy gate reads), the derived string-to-family map, and the ontology scan's JSON from tools/ontology_qa/quality.py --json.
  • Tools, read-only and shipping with the release: tools/query/kg_query.py (the query engine — writes nothing, labels every derived row, prints the graph state and seed digest it ran against) and tools/query/export_rdf.py (the Turtle export, which declares no transitivity).

UI

  • Pages added in this release: release notes, a page per relation family, a page per batch, the query panel and the ontology page. The ledger gained facets, record permalinks and an assignment-history chip; the batch narrative moved off it onto the per-batch pages.
  • Design rules adopted, all in the V2 section of docs/ATLAS_REQUIREMENTS.md: one site menu with a fixed taxonomy (V2-R10), a pinned header with constant geometry (V2-R11), one title band and one search position (V2-R12), two content-column kinds that do not wander (V2-R13), progressive disclosure on heavy pages (V2-R9), volumes in collection order (V2-R8), and per-batch delta pages (V2-R7).
  • Deliberately not exposed: the depth-bounded identity walk. It is built and tested in tools/query/kg_query.py and documented in docs/QUERY.md, and it is kept for later rather than shipped in this release's UI.
  • Nothing in this set is deployed by the build that made it. A deploy carrying UI waits for review.
