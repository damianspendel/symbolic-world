v1.4: the alchemical trilogy, the archetype volumes, and the relation-family layer

pending. The ceremony waits on the arXiv publication of the paper. Scope: the 2026-08-05 plan put Aion and CW 9i in v1.5; the arcs closed first, so they are here.
graph v1.4 · ruleset v0.7.2 · calibration record pipeline/family_calibration/records/2026-08-13_v072_run4.json (PASS)

mining status

  • Mined CW 12, 13 and 14; the alchemical trilogy is complete.
  • Mined Aion (§1–429), closed at b-cw9ii-7 on 2026-08-06.
  • Mined CW 9i (§1–718) over ten windows, closed at b-cw9i-10 on 2026-08-12.
  • Graph: 532 nodes, 1934 relations, 2430 references over 61 merged batches, all on the ledger.
  • Normalized 260 relation strings; the verbatim string stays frozen beside each.
  • Assigned families: 1549 assigned, 328 queued, 55 no family, 2 text undecided (assigned, not verified; families).
  • Found the clause-predicate defect at b-cw12-1: 271 relations carrying a proposition in the predicate.
  • Traced it to a briefing bullet; replaced the bullet with a form rule at b-cw9ii-3. Fifteen blind batches at 0% since.
  • Did not re-mine: each affected relation is verified true, so it carries a normalization layer instead.

governance

  • Moved the ruleset to v0.7.2, 25 families; every version carries a calibration PASS record and CI refuses a version without one.
  • Re-ran every amplified-by edge under ruling 1; amplification now holds none; the page carries the count.
  • Declared 7 inverse and symmetric pairs, each citing a document line. No transitivity.
  • Calibrated the family gate: 19/19 planted errors caught, 39/41 clean assignments left alone (measures the instrument, not Jung). Gate model gemini-3.1-pro-preview: within-vendor role separation, not cross-vendor.
  • Checked 27 assignments against 8 works over 3 passes: 8/16 treated items agreed, 6 disagreed, 2 split, 6 untreated; 7 rules changed. Receipts in docs/experiments/external_validity_2026-08/.
  • Withdrew four reviewer-supplied citations (one held) and the pre-August calibration figures.
  • Flagged b-cw9ii-1 against b-cw9ii-2 as confounded: the blinding changed between them.

core build

  • Launched the site on Cloudflare Pages, 2026-08-07.
  • Adopted extensionless URLs, 2026-08-10.
  • Made the generators deterministic.
  • Emitted /graph.json, the declarations, the batch manifest, the family map and the ontology scan.
  • Shipped tools/query/kg_query.py and tools/query/export_rdf.py, read-only.

UI

  • Added releases, ontology, query, a page per relation family, a page per batch.
  • Added facets, record permalinks and the assignment-history chip to the ledger; moved the batch narrative onto the batch pages.
  • Adopted the design rules recorded as V2-R7 to V2-R13 in docs/ATLAS_REQUIREMENTS.md.
