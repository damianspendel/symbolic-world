released 2026-09-05. Tag v2.0.0, GitHub Release, Zenodo record 10.5281/zenodo.22339291 under the concept DOI; the v1.3 record is marked superseded and retained. graph v2.0 · ruleset v0.7.2 · calibration record pipeline/family_calibration/records/2026-08-13_v072_run4.json (PASS)

mining status

  • Mined the trilogy (CW 12, 13, 14), Aion to b-cw9ii-7, CW 9i to b-cw9i-10, and CW 8 to b-cw8-14 (2026-08-28): five volumes end to end.
  • Graph: 661 nodes, 2463 relations, 3248 references over 74 merged batches, all on the ledger; 260 relations normalized, the verbatim string frozen beside each.
  • Families: 1941 assigned, 434 queued, 85 no family, 3 text undecided (families).
  • Found the clause-predicate defect at b-cw12-1 (271 relations with a proposition in the predicate), traced it to a briefing bullet, and replaced the bullet with a form rule at b-cw9ii-3; every blind batch since ran at 0%. No re-mine: the relations are verified true and carry the normalization layer; reuse per window is in pipeline/transfer_trend.jsonl.

governance

  • Moved the ruleset to v0.7.2, 25 families; every version carries a PASS record and tools/check_paper_facts.py refuses one without it.
  • Re-ran every amplified-by edge under ruling 1; amplification now holds none; the page carries the count.
  • Declared 7 inverse and symmetric pairs, each citing its ruleset line.
  • Calibrated the family gate: 19/19 planted errors caught, 39/41 clean left alone. Gate model gemini-3.1-pro-preview: within-vendor role separation, not cross-vendor.
  • Checked 27 assignments against 8 works: 8/16 treated agreed, 6 disagreed, 2 split; 7 rules changed. Receipts in docs/experiments/external_validity_2026-08/.
  • Withdrew four reviewer citations (one held) and the pre-August calibration figures; flagged b-cw9ii-1 vs b-cw9ii-2 as confounded.
  • Added release checkpoints: ledger freshness, batch manifest, size budget, the copy contract, and the poster fact check, tools/check_poster_facts.py (the image's numbers are pixels; preflight compares its sidecar to seed.json).

core build

  • Launched on Cloudflare Pages 2026-08-07; extensionless URLs 2026-08-10; deterministic generators; emitted /graph.json, the declarations, the batch manifest, the family map and the ontology scan.
  • Shipped the query engine (tools/query/kg_query.py, read-only) with read-time closure and the Turtle export; no transitivity anywhere.

UI

  • Added releases, ontology, query, a page per family and per batch; facets, permalinks and the assignment-history chip on the ledger; the taxonomy and rules V2-R7 to V2-R13 in docs/ATLAS_REQUIREMENTS.md.
  • Added in a nutshell, the method poster on its own page; the identity walk stays in kg_query.py, not exposed on the site.
