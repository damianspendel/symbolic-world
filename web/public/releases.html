Releases

What changed between one release of the graph and the next, and why. The ledger records every individual claim and the gates it passed; this file records the release-level story: the graph, the ruleset, the instruments, what was checked outside this repository, and what was corrected or withdrawn.

Every entry opens with a version pin. A graph release and a ruleset version are deliberately separate numbers — a release pins the ruleset it ships with rather than sharing its number — and the pin names the calibration record that was binding at tag time.

This file carries no typed figure. Counts are written as named tokens in double braces and bound from the graph, the calibration records and the committed experiment receipts by web/build-atlas.mjs; a token the build cannot bind fails the build rather than shipping as literal braces.

the graph

  • Five arcs mined end to end since v1.3.0: Psychology and Alchemy (CW 12), Alchemical Studies (CW 13) and Mysterium Coniunctionis (CW 14) complete the alchemical trilogy; Aion (CW 9ii §1–429) closed on 2026-08-06 with b-cw9ii-7; The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious (CW 9i §1–718) closed on 2026-08-12 with b-cw9i-10, ten windows, every reference proposed by an Opus-family model, gated by a Fable-family model and audited across vendors by Gemini.
  • 407 nodes, 1555 edges and 1847 references over 51 merged batch entries. Every reference carries a verbatim quote of at most 25 tokens and the paragraph it came from; the per-batch pages carry each batch's own account of what it merged.
  • CW 9i was mined as the transfer study's falsification volume: archetype theory without alchemical carriers, against a graph in which a large share of nodes had rested on the trilogy alone. What that did to the evidence mix is on each node's panel in the atlas, not summarised into a claim here.
  • Relation strings normalized on 260 edges. The verbatim string stays frozen beside the normalized one, and the family layer classifies the normalized form where it exists.
  • The family layer covers the graph: 1250 assigned, 262 waiting on a human ruling, 41 that no family covers, 2 where the sentence itself hedges. An assignment classifies a relation that was already gated against its paragraph. It is not a second verification, and nothing on this site calls a family verified.

the clause-predicate defect, and the form rule

  • The windowed pipeline built after v1.3.0 shipped carried no rule about the shape of a relation. Every gate asked whether the paragraph supported the claim; none asked whether the predicate was a predicate. The result was 271 relations whose predicate carries a whole proposition — found, named and measured on 2026-08-05, starting exactly at b-cw12-1.
  • The mechanism was a line in the miner's own standing briefing instructing that qualifiers stay inside the relation string. A watched batch tidied its relations in spite of it; the blind b-cw9ii-2 followed it literally and came back at 77% clause predicates. That is a defect that propagated through the instruction channel, not through the model.
  • The bullet was replaced with a form rule — the relation is a verb phrase of at most three hyphen tokens; carrier, scope, condition and modality belong in qualifiers or the object. b-cw9ii-3 was the test, and every blind batch since has run at 0% clause predicates: fifteen consecutive by the close of CW 9i on 2026-08-12.
  • The affected relations were not re-mined. Each is individually verified and true — the shape is wrong, not the fact — so they carry a normalization layer beside the frozen original instead, proposed, gated and sampled through the same chain as any other claim.

ruleset

  • docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md reached v0.7.1, holding 25 families. The version is binding only while a calibration PASS record for it is committed; CI refuses a version without one.
  • The re-run of the amplified-by edges under ruling 1 classified those relations by the content of their quotes rather than by the layer tag they carried. Where the amplification family now stands is on its own page, count included, whatever that count has become.
  • 7 relation declarations are active — inverse and symmetric pairs, each transcribed from a line of the definitions document and applied at read time only. No declaration asserts transitivity.

instruments and calibration

  • The family gate was re-calibrated against planted errors under v0.7.1: 19 of 19 planted errors caught with the right family named, 40 of 41 untouched assignments left alone. A caught planted error is evidence about the instrument, not about Jung, and the record covers only the error classes we know how to plant.
  • The gate model is gemini-3.1-pro-preview — the same model family as one of the two production classification arms. That is within-vendor role separation, not a cross-vendor check, and the record says so.
  • tools/query/kg_query.py ships read-only. It writes nothing, labels every derived row, and prints the graph state and seed digest it ran against.

checked outside the repository

  • The external-validity check closed on 2026-08-12 over 3 passes against 8 works of secondary scholarship. Of 16 sampled assignments the literature treats, scholars' renderings agreed with our family on 8, disagreed on 6 and split among themselves on 2; 6 pairs no consulted work treats at all.
  • 16 treated pairs is a small sample, and agreement there is not endorsement: no scholar reviewed this vocabulary. The disagreements produced 7 rule changes and are published beside the agreements, family by family. The sample, the per-item worksheets and both review passes are committed under docs/experiments/external_validity_2026-08/.

published

  • The site went live on Cloudflare Pages on 2026-08-07 at symbolicworld.observer: the atlas, the ledger, the dispute log and the families document, with the frozen v1.3.0 snapshot preserved at /v1.3/ and linked from the landing page, since the paper describes that release and not this one.
  • Public URLs are extensionless by convention from 2026-08-10; the sitemap, the canonical tags and every internal link agree on that form.
  • This release adds the release notes, a page per relation family, a page per batch, the query panel and the ontology page. Nothing in that set is deployed by the build that made it.

corrected or withdrawn

  • Four treatments supplied by a reviewing model in the check's first pass were put to the actual texts: one held, one was retracted, and two named headwords the source does not contain. None entered the headline rates. They are named here because they appeared in a draft that was circulated.
  • Calibration figures published before August 2026 were withdrawn when a code inspection found they could not be substantiated. The sections that carried them stand in the definitions document as a record of what was done, not as current evidence.
  • The b-cw9ii-1 and b-cw9ii-2 comparison is confounded: the blinding of the miner changed between them, so the pair is not clean evidence about the material, and the 77% figure above is reported as what that batch did rather than as a measurement of anything else.
