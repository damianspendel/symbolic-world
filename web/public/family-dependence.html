Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 23. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

Requirement, precondition, subordination, rulership: one term needs, presupposes or is governed by the other — a STANDING condition.

Boundary: the dependence rules above absorb conditional "precedes" and blocking-phrased requirements (bars-gateway-to).
