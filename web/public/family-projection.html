Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 22. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

Psychic contents relocated into an outer carrier — the central explanatory mechanism for alchemy. Includes withdrawal edges.

Boundary: not symbolization (a symbol formulates; a projection relocates and is later withdrawn); not manifestation (the archetype showing itself is not relocated by a psyche).
