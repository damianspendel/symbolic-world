Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 10. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

The terms are joined, married, fused — the coniunctio class, central enough in this corpus to stand apart from composition.

Boundary: an act or achieved state of joining distinct terms, vs composition's standing parthood; three-place unions ("A unites B[-and-C]") keep the united pair in object or qualifiers.
