Disputes

Challenged claims, with the objection and the outcome. Challenges come from our own cross-vendor audits and from readers. Upheld disputes are corrected or removed; both-defensible cases are documented and left unchanged; not-upheld objections are answered.

To dispute an edge, open its citation in the atlas, press dispute, and file the report as a GitHub issue. A dispute must say what the cited paragraph says; reports that do not are closed as incomplete.

To confirm an edge, check the cited paragraph in any edition and press confirm. Confirmations enter the edge's public verification record.
