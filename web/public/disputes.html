Disputes

Every challenged connection, the objection raised, and the published outcome — whether the challenge came from our own cross-vendor audits or from a reader. This log is the falsification offer working in public: upheld disputes are corrected or removed; both-defensible cases are documented without change; not-upheld objections are answered. To dispute an edge, open its citation in the atlas, use the dispute button, and submit the report as a GitHub issue. Admissibility is itself gated: a dispute must state what the cited paragraph actually says — reports that do not engage the paragraph are closed as incomplete, without adjudication.

Readers can also confirm edges: check the cited paragraph in any edition and submit a confirmation via the atlas's confirm button — human confirmations enter the edge's public verification record. The machine gates are the floor; readers are the ceiling.
