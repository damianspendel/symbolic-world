The opus

Jung's reading of the ten pictures of the Rosarium philosophorum.

The Rosarium philosophorum (Frankfurt, 1550) tells the alchemical work in ten woodcuts. Jung reads them in order in "The Psychology of the Transference" (CW 16). Each picture below has citations from the graph. Colours adjust as the sequence progresses.

cauda pavonis, the peacock's tail

Picture 1 · CW 16 §402 to §409

The Mercurial Fountain

  • Alchemy derives from The unconscious

    “the methods and philosophy of alchemy ... they can only derive from the unconscious psyche”

    CW 16 §407 · Jung asserts

  • Quintessence (quinta essentia) derives from The four elements

    “a fifth star which represents the fifth entity, the “One” derived from the four, the quinta essentia”

    CW 16 §402 · Jung reports a parallel

  • Mercurius is counterpart of The Trinity

    “he is a trinity, triunus or trinus , the chthonic, lower, or even infernal counterpart of the Heavenly Trinity”

    CW 16 §403 · Jung reports a parallel

Picture 2 · CW 16 §410 to §449

King and Queen

  • The unconscious produces The symbol

    “these symbolical pictures spring from the corresponding unconscious contents—half spiritual, half sexual”

    CW 16 §414 · Jung asserts

  • Regina (the Queen) represents The artifex (alchemist-operator)

    Rex (the King) represents Soror mystica

    “the man (in this case the adept) is represented by the queen, and the woman (the soror mystica ) by the king”

    CW 16 §421 · Jung asserts

  • Coniunctio involves Incest (symbolic)

    “the desired coniunctio was not a legitimate union but was always—one could almost say, on principleincestuous”

    CW 16 §415 · Jung asserts

Picture 3 · CW 16 §450 to §452

The Naked Truth

  • The alchemical opus (opus alchymicum) requires Morality

    “the opus demands not only intellectual and technical ability ... it is a moral as well as a psychological undertaking”

    CW 16 §451 · Jung asserts

    “He who would be initiated into this art and secret wisdom must put away the vice of arrogance, must be devout, righteous”

    CW 16 §450 · Jung quotes a source

  • Coniunctio amplified by Sol

    Coniunctio amplified by Luna

    “Sol says, O Luna, let me be thy husband”

    CW 16 §451 · Jung quotes a source

  • Repression leads to Dissociation (splitting of the personality)

    “Repression leads to a one-sided development if not to stagnation, and eventually to neurotic dissociation.”

    CW 16 §452 · Jung asserts

Picture 4 · CW 16 §453 to §456

Immersion in the Bath

  • Bath is form of The Night Sea Journey

    “The immersion in the bath is another “night sea journey,””

    CW 16 §455 · Jung asserts

  • The rotundum (the round element) corresponds to Wholeness / totality

    “Wholeness is a combination of I and You ... whose nature can only be grasped symbolically, as in the symbols of the rotundum”

    CW 16 §454 · Jung asserts

  • Wholeness / totality requires Soul

    “The unrelated human being lacks wholeness, for he can achieve wholeness only through the soul”

    CW 16 §454 · Jung asserts

Picture 5 · CW 16 §457 to §466

The Conjunction

  • Nous is embraced by Physis

    “Physis has wrapped the “man of light” in a passionate embrace.”

    CW 16 §457 · Jung asserts

  • Water symbolizes The unconscious

    “the coitus is taking place in the water, the mare tenebrositatis , i.e., the unconscious”

    CW 16 §459 · Jung asserts

  • Coniunctio is a Hierosgamos (sacred marriage / chymical wedding)

    “It is evident from this that the coniunctio of Sol and Mercurius is a hierosgamos, with Mercurius playing the role of bride.”

    CW 14 §120 · Jung asserts

Picture 6 · CW 16 §467 to §474

Death

The page turns black where Jung names the nigredo. CW 16 §468.

  • Death precedes Rebirth / Renovatio

    “an indication that this death is an interim stage to be followed by a new life”

    CW 16 §467 · Jung asserts

  • Coniunctio followed by Nigredo

    “The coniunctio was incestuous...The nigredo always appears in conjunction with tenebrositas”

    CW 16 §468 · Jung asserts

  • Sol niger (black sun) belongs to Nigredo

    “Hence there is also a Sol niger, a black sun, which coincides with the nigredo and putrefactio, the state of death.”

    CW 14 §113 · Jung reports a parallel

Picture 7 · CW 16 §475 to §482

The Ascent of the Soul

The black holds. CW 16 §479.

  • Dissociation (splitting of the personality) is analogous to Schizophrenia (dementia praecox)

    “The decomposition of the elements indicates dissociation and the collapse of the existing ego-consciousness. It is closely analogous to the schizophrenic state”

    CW 16 §476 · Jung asserts

  • Mercurius resists integration into Consciousness (ego, diurnal psyche)

    “Mercurius, the servus or cervus fugitivus who is ever about to flee—or who, in other words, resists integration (into consciousness)”

    CW 16 §478 · Jung asserts

  • Nigredo parallels Dark night of the soul

    “allows us to adduce as a parallel to the nigredo the writings of St. John of the Cross 7 concerning the “dark night.””

    CW 16 §479 · Jung asserts

“Afterwards thou makest new and multitudinous things to grow, causing with thy verdure the many colours to appear.”CW 16 §479 · the Rosarium, quoted by Jung · a quote outside the graph, checked against its paragraph
“The appearance of the colours in the alchemical vessel, the so-called cauda pavonis, denotes the spring, the renewal of life”CW 16 §480 · Jung asserts · a quote outside the graph, checked against its paragraph

Picture 8 · CW 16 §483 to §493

Purification

The black fades to white. Jung names the whitening at CW 16 §484, and it is complete at §493. CW 16 §493.

  • Water symbolizes Sapientia / Wisdom

    “water as a symbol of wisdom and spirit can be traced back to the parable which Christ told to the Samaritan woman at the well”

    CW 16 §485 · Jung asserts

  • Dew belongs to Albedo

    “the dew falls from heaven, purifies the body, and makes it ready to receive the soul; 195 in other words, it brings about the albedo”

    CW 14 §155 · Jung reports a parallel

  • Nigredo precedes Albedo

    “the nigredo gives way to the albedo”

    CW 16 §493 · Jung asserts

Picture 9 · CW 16 §494 to §524

The Return of the Soul

White. CW 16 §515.

  • The artifex (alchemist-operator) focuses on The unconscious

    “on the part of the alchemist ... his main interest is focussed on the unconscious itself”

    CW 16 §498 · Jung asserts

  • Unio mentalis precedes Reunion with the body

    “The second step on the way to the production of this substance was the reunion of the spirit with the body.”

    CW 14 §677 · Jung reports a parallel

  • Nigredo precedes Albedo

    “They call it their resurrection, because the white rises up out of the black”

    CW 16 §515 · Jung quotes a source

Picture 10 · CW 16 §525 to §539

The New Birth

Jung names no stage in these paragraphs; the white holds as a choice of display, not a claim.

  • Anthropos / Adam Kadmon parallels Christ (as symbol)

    “the Platonic conception of the bisexual First Man, for Christ is ultimately the Anthropos”

    CW 16 §525 · Jung asserts

  • Denarius (the number ten) means Christ (as symbol)

    “Hence the real meaning of the denarius is the Son of God.”

    CW 16 §525 · Jung asserts

  • Rebis / Hermaphrodite symbolizes Union of opposites

    “He reconciles the two “in one body,” 54 an idea which is figuratively represented in alchemy as the two-headed hermaphrodite.”

    CW 14 §11 · Jung asserts

After the white

The white turns yellow, then red. CW 12 §271.

“The albedo is, so to speak, the daybreak, but not till the rubedo is it sunrise.”CW 12 §334 · Jung asserts · a quote outside the graph, checked against its paragraph

  • Citrinitas (yellowing) coincides with Rubedo

    “In alchemy yellow ( citrinitas ) often coincides with the rubedo .”

    CW 12 §271 · Jung reports a parallel

  • Coniunctio belongs to Rubedo

    “The alchemists termed this the rubedo, in which the marriage of the red man and the white woman, Sol and Luna, is consummated”

    CW 14 §307 · Jung reports a parallel
