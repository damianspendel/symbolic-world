What it merged

Spirit and Life closes into Basic Postulates of Analytical Psychology, which closes into the opening of Analytical Psychology and a Weltanschauung. §642-648: spirit as an autonomous complex with an intention superior to the ego's, felt as a wider rather than higher consciousness, expressible only through a symbol once it exceeds concept, and the reciprocal limit where life is the touchstone of a spirit's truth. §649-688: the historical swing from a metaphysics of mind to a metaphysics of matter, the Zeitgeist as an irrational creed working through the unconscious, "psychology without the psyche" as its product, the etymological recovery of soul as moving force and breath, the primitive experience of the psyche as objective and self-subsistent, the collective unconscious as an inherited system older than consciousness, and psychic reality as the explanatory ground ending the mind/matter deadlock, closing on the clinical stakes of a one-sidedly naturalistic or spiritual reading. §689-712: Weltanschauung as an attitude formulated into concepts, its retroactive grip on the man who holds it, a critique of Freudian psychoanalysis as rationalistic materialism reducing dream and art to symptom, and the autonomous complex closing case — a mother-complex where insight dissolves the symptom but not the longing, and the primitive's projection of autonomous contents onto spirits, materialism and mysticism named as hostile brothers.
