Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.2, family 14, added at v0.2 — review finding 13. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

Doctrinal, historical or textual transmission: one tradition, doctrine, text or figure draws from, descends from, influences or supplants another.

Boundary: generation begets beings; derivation transmits ideas and forms. The RELATION itself must assert transmission (draws from, descends from, influences, supplants, continues). Subject type is evidence, never a default — a tradition-subject edge whose relation asserts telos, privation, expression or opposition takes THAT family (alchemy reached-its-summit-in faust → telos; privatio-boni makes-evil-diminution-of good → privation; dogma expresses collective-unconscious → symbolization).
