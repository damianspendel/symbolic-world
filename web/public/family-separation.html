Definition

Quoted from docs/FAMILY_DEFINITIONS.md v0.7.1, family 11, added at v0.2 — review finding 11; reworked at v0.3. The atlas quotes that document; it never paraphrases it.

The separatio/solutio class, in BOTH its structures: (i) A is parted, extracted or freed from B; (ii) A dissolves, separates or splits B. The opus's counter-operation to union and as central.

Boundary. not transformation (where the subject BECOMES the object — so messiah splits-into the-opposites, whose quote says the split "becomes a complete polarity", is transformation-toward-opposition and goes per edge, not here); not psychic-effect (separation asserts the parting itself, not a dynamic residue — this is a definitional distinction, not just check-order).
