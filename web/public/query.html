Query the graph

The filters tools/query/kg_query.py takes, given a surface. They combine with AND, exactly as the tool combines them, and each one reads a field the graph already stores rather than computing a judgement of its own. The closure toggle adds the rows the active declarations license and marks every one of them as derived.

Nothing on this page writes anything. The panel reads the published dataset at /graph.json and the declarations beside it; the same query on the command line prints the same rows with a footer naming the graph it ran against. Two results carrying different seed digests are not comparable, which is why the digest sits under the results rather than in a help page.

Identity walk

Walk over archetypal-identity edges. Each hop below is ONE edge, printed with its own citation. No transitive identity is asserted: a term reached in two hops is not thereby identical to the start. FAMILY_DEFINITIONS v0.7.1 rejects transitivity on every family (line 131, the uroboros argument); this walk is exploration of what the graph connects, not inference over it.

A derived row is not an edge. It carries its source edge's references and no citation of its own, it is not written to the graph, and it does not compound: closure is applied once to the stored edges and never to its own output. A count of rows is therefore not a count of edges, and the line under the results says which is which.
